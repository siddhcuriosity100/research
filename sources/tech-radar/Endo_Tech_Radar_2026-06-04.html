Endo Founder Tech Radar

Market & technology intelligence for Endo Automation — June 04, 2026

Editor's Note

This run is a clean reset onto the founder schema — 16 market players and deals across Endo's space, all NEW. The single most important development for the founder: Varaha's $20M (first tranche of a $45M) Series B led by WestBridge (Feb 2026) crowns an India-rooted soil-carbon MRV leader with Google/Microsoft offtake — a partner-not-competitor whose biggest credibility gap (shallow, satellite-modeled sampling) is exactly the ground-truth that cheap Endo sensors can fill. On the build-vs-buy front, three connectivity/edge enablers — Blues, Memfault (now Nordic) and DEEPX — all score 14/15 and let Endo buy its plumbing instead of building it.

Movers & Money

Soil & agri sensing

CropX Technologiescompany / platformNEW
Capability 5Ease 3Momentum 4Combined 12/15

Israel-based soil-sensing and digital-agronomy company whose in-ground probes measure moisture, temperature, salinity (and, via acquired tech, nitrate) and feed an AI agronomy/irrigation platform. In March 2025 it launched 'Apex', a multi-depth sensor reading the full root-zone profile at 4-inch intervals.

Backers & Funding: Founders incl. Tomer Tzach (CEO); investors incl. Finistere Ventures, Innovation Endeavors (Eric Schmidt), GreenSoil Investments, OurCrowd, Aliaxis, ICL Group, Flashpoint VC. ~$90.8M total raised across ~10 rounds from ~45-47 investors. Latest disclosed equity rounds predate 2025; recent activity is M&A (acquired Acclym/Agritask Sept 2025; EnGeniousAg nitrogen sensor 2024). Latest fresh-round size/date: n/a.

Traction: Deployed in 50-70+ countries; ~1,200+ paying customers and ~8,500 installations since 2017. Reported ~40% water savings and ~10% yield uplift. Seven acquisitions consolidating a full-stack soil-to-agronomy platform.

Reviews: Positive: praised for data-transmission reliability, sensor accuracy and easy DIY probe install; seen as a fast-growing consolidator. Negative: customers cite weak agronomy support after the sale; platform is broad/enterprise-leaning rather than low-cost; aggressive roll-up raises integration questions.

Pricing: Hardware sensor + paid SaaS subscription (per-sensor/per-field annual fee); enterprise tiers. List pricing not public (n/a).

Endo take: Best-in-class incumbent and a partner/benchmark rather than a buy target; their multi-depth Apex is what a low-cost Indian sensor stack is measured against. Opportunity to partner on agronomy, threat if they push downmarket into India.

Stenon (FarmLab)company / productNEW
Capability 4Ease 3Momentum 2Combined 9/15

German maker of FarmLab, a handheld spade-style device delivering real-time, in-situ soil analysis (mineral nitrogen, organic matter/SOC, moisture, temperature, texture) in seconds via optical spectroscopy + impedance sensing and AI, eliminating lab sampling.

Backers & Funding: Founders incl. Dominic Roth & Niels Grabbert; investors incl. Founders Fund, The Production Board, Cherry Ventures, Atlantic Labs, Oyster Bay. ~$21.2M total: $20M Series A (Dec 2021, Founders Fund / The Production Board) + ~$1.05M seed. No new round disclosed 2024-2026 (latest round: n/a since 2021).

Traction: Commercial deployments across Europe and Brazil (potato and row-crop growers); assembled in Germany with TUV and DLG certifications. Rental/usage model lowers adoption friction. Revenue figures not disclosed (n/a).

Reviews: Positive: DLG-certified for mobile soil-tester accuracy; strong on physical parameters and a novel instant-NPK pitch. Negative: peer-reviewed 2024-25 field studies found poor temporal consistency and low correlation for mineral nitrogen, SOC and especially phosphorus — 'not yet suitable for precision nutrient management' per one study.

Pricing: Device-as-a-service / rental rather than outright sale; per-use or subscription. Exact pricing not public (n/a).

Endo take: Closest technical analogue to Endo's in-field sensing thesis and a useful capability benchmark — validates the market but flags the hard accuracy ceiling on optical NPK/SOC. Mostly a learning reference and possible partner.

Soil carbon / MRV

Varahacompany / platformNEW
Capability 4Ease 4Momentum 5Combined 13/15

Gurugram, India-based carbon-removal developer and MRV platform combining remote sensing, ML and field science to quantify and verify regenerative-agriculture, agroforestry, biochar and enhanced-rock-weathering credits across the Global South.

Backers & Funding: Founders Madhur Jain (CEO), Ankita Garg, Vishal Kuchanur. Series B led by WestBridge Capital (its first climate-tech bet); existing investors RTP Global and Omnivore participating. $20M first tranche of a targeted $45M Series B, led by WestBridge Capital (announced Feb 3, 2026), with RTP Global and Omnivore. Prior Series A ~$8.7M. Revenue Rs430M FY25, forecast Rs2B FY26.

Traction: ~170,000-175,000 farmers across ~1.7M acres in India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan and Ivory Coast; >2M tonnes CO2 removed across 14 projects, ~150,000 credits issued. Offtake deals with Google, Microsoft, Lufthansa, Swiss Re, Capgemini.

Reviews: Positive: credible Global South MRV leader with marquee buyers and a 90%-cost-cut remote-sensing pitch; WestBridge entry seen as strong validation. Negative: sector under heavy scrutiny — rice/methane and soil-carbon credits face additionality/quality concerns and questions about shallow (<30cm) sampling rigor; tied to a volatile voluntary carbon market.

Pricing: Project-developer model: shares credit revenue with farmers, sells verified credits to corporates; MRV is the verification backbone. Per-credit pricing varies (n/a).

Endo take: Highest-momentum India-rooted soil-carbon player and a natural partner/channel — Endo's low-cost soil sensors and ETP/STP carbon hardware could feed Varaha's MRV pipeline rather than compete. Strong build-vs-partner case to supply ground-truth measurement.

Boomitracompany / marketplaceNEW
Capability 4Ease 4Momentum 4Combined 12/15

California-based (India-rooted founder) soil-carbon MRV and credit marketplace using satellite imagery + AI to estimate soil organic carbon, cutting field-sampling costs ~90%, and issuing verified credits for smallholders across the Global South. 2023 Earthshot Prize winner.

Backers & Funding: Founder/CEO Aadith Moorthy. Investors incl. Yara Growth Ventures (lead), Chevron Technology Ventures, Global Innovation Fund, Jerry Yang, Tom Steyer and Kat Taylor. ~$11M total raised (incl. ~$4M 2021 round led by Yara; Global Innovation Fund equity). No large new priced round disclosed 2025-26 (latest round: n/a). Revenue driven by credit sales.

Traction: 150,000+ farmers (100,000+ verified) across ~5M acres on four continents; ~10M tonnes CO2 removed. India 'SOVIN' project: 88,000 acres, 23,000+ farmers, Verra VM0042; first 47,000 credits issued. Landmark 500,000-credit deal with Restoration Climate / Ethereum Climate Platform.

Reviews: Positive: Earthshot Prize and TIME100 recognition; lauded for satellite-only low-cost MRV and returning ~70% of credit revenue to farmers. Negative: satellite-modeled carbon draws methodological skepticism on accuracy; exposed to the soil-carbon-credit 'trough of disillusionment' and price softness; equity funding modest vs ambitions.

Pricing: Marketplace model — sells verified credits to corporate/government buyers; farmers receive ~70%. No per-credit list price (n/a).

Endo take: Strong India-active MRV partner whose satellite-only approach is precisely where cheap ground-truth soil sensors add credibility — Endo hardware could de-risk Boomitra's biggest critique. Clear partner/opportunity, not a competitor.

Electrochemical / soil sensors

Yard Stick PBCcompanyNEW
Capability 4Ease 3Momentum 5Combined 12/15

Handheld spectrometer probe that measures soil organic carbon stocks in situ (validated to ~45cm depth) in seconds, replacing slow, costly lab combustion testing for carbon MRV.

Backers & Funding: Investors include Toyota Ventures Climate Fund, Microsoft Climate Innovation Fund, The Nature Conservancy, Lowercarbon Capital, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Pillar VC; plus ARPA-E grant support. $10.6M Series A (2023, led by Toyota Ventures Climate Fund) plus a new $18M round announced July 2025 (~$28M+ disclosed total).

Traction: Field-deployed across carbon-program acreage; published a peer-reviewed accuracy study with the Soil Health Institute validating in-field carbon measurement to ~45cm. Positioned as the leading low-cost soil-carbon MRV hardware play.

Reviews: Positive: reputation strong in the soil-carbon/MRV community, bolstered by independent SHI validation (a credibility differentiator most sensor startups lack). Negative: value tightly coupled to volatile voluntary carbon-credit markets; less proven for general agronomy/NPK use.

Pricing: n/a publicly — sells measurement-as-a-service into carbon programs rather than commodity hardware.

Endo take: Most relevant to Endo's soil-carbon hardware ambition — a validated, well-funded benchmark for low-cost in-field SOC measurement and a potential partner/reference design. Their SHI-validated accuracy bar is the standard Endo must hit to be credible in carbon MRV.

TerraBlastercompanyNEW
Capability 5Ease 2Momentum 4Combined 11/15

Stealth-to-public agtech building rugged Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS) sensors that drag behind a tractor/ATV ~6 inches deep to map pH and NPK (plus micronutrients) in real time, aiming to replace slow, sparse lab soil sampling.

Backers & Funding: Founders Jorge Heraud (ex-Trimble precision-ag lead; Blue River Technology co-founder, sold to John Deere for $305M) and Matt Colgan (ex-Blue River/See & Spray). Investors: Khosla Ventures (lead), Trailhead Capital, Bidra/OCP Group VC arm, The Reservoir. ~$7M total disclosed: a $3M seed (as 'SoilCode' pre-rebrand) plus an oversubscribed $4M pre-seed led by Khosla Ventures (announced August 2025). Now raising a $10-15M seed to scale.

Traction: Pre-commercial: moved from concept to field-validated prototype; commercial launch targeted late 2026. No paying customers yet. Strong industry signal via Heraud's pedigree.

Reviews: No user reviews (not shipping). Industry reception strong — covered as a standout 2025 agtech launch; Heraud calls it 'the number one opportunity in agtech.' Skepticism centers on whether LIBS hardware can be miniaturized/cheapened enough and survive harsh field conditions at tractor speed.

Pricing: n/a — hardware price still being finalized; stated goal is to not exceed what farmers currently pay for conventional soil sampling.

Endo take: Direct capability threat in real-time NPK sensing, but LIBS is high-cost/high-power and aimed at large mechanized US/EU farms — not Endo's low-cost smallholder niche, so more a tech north-star than a buy/partner. Watch their cost trajectory closely.

IoT device & fleet platforms

Capability 4Ease 5Momentum 5Combined 14/15

Maker of the Notecard — a prepaid cellular/Wi-Fi/LoRa/satellite IoT module — paired with Notehub, a secure cloud data-routing and device-management service. Drop-in connectivity-plus-cloud for hardware builders.

Backers & Funding: Founded by Ray Ozzie (ex-Microsoft CTO, Lotus Notes creator). Latest round led by Sequoia Capital (Roelof Botha joined the board). $25M round led by Sequoia Capital, announced May 20, 2025; substantial prior venture funding (total in the tens of millions).

Traction: Broad developer/maker and industrial adoption; expanded global satellite coverage with Starnote for Iridium (Sept 2025) for fallback connectivity in remote areas. Sequoia lead and Botha board seat are strong scale signals.

Reviews: Positive: developer sentiment notably good — transparent no-subscription 'freemium' consumption pricing, simple integration, global connectivity. Negative: usage-based event pricing can be hard to forecast at scale, and you pay on data egress from Notehub.

Pricing: Notecard hardware ~$49 (lower-cost ~$10 Wi-Fi option); Notehub freemium — 5,000 free events/month, then ~$0.00075 per event (charged on egress).

Endo take: Strongest buy-vs-build candidate for Endo's connectivity layer — a Sequoia-backed, satellite-capable module that could connect rural Indian soil/ETP sensors off-the-shelf, though per-Notecard BOM (~$49) may be too high for ultra-low-cost smallholder units. Prototype with it, evaluate cost at scale.

End-to-end embedded observability, fleet health monitoring, and secure OTA firmware-update platform for connected devices — lets hardware teams catch, triage and fix field bugs and push updates remotely.

Edge AI

DEEPXcompanyNEW
Capability 5Ease 4Momentum 5Combined 14/15

South Korean fabless startup making low-power edge NPUs (DX-M1, 25 TOPS at 1-5W) for on-device AI in robotics, cameras and industrial/IoT devices. Available as cheap M.2/HAT modules for Raspberry Pi and x86/ARM hosts.

Backers & Funding: Founder/CEO Lokwon Kim (ex-Apple, Cisco, IBM); investors SkyLake Equity Partners (lead), BNW Investments, AJU IB, TimeFolio Asset Management. $80.5M Series C at a $529M valuation (May 2024) led by SkyLake; cumulative private funding ~$531M. Hired Morgan Stanley in 2025 for a pre-IPO round; targeting an IPO around 2027.

Traction: Mass production began mid-2025; ~350-company PoC pipeline and 27 confirmed purchase orders across 8 countries within 7 months. Distributed via Avnet, DigiKey, WPG; module partners Radxa, Sixfab, DFRobot; production partners AAEON, Advantech, Hyundai Robotics.

Reviews: Positive: strong perf-per-watt (25 TOPS at ~3W), low module cost, supports TensorFlow/PyTorch/ONNX and modern models (YOLO, ViT, VLMs). Negative: young toolchain still maturing, heavy reliance on partner integration vs turnkey, and limited direct India presence so support/localization unproven.

Pricing: Hardware sales: Sixfab AI HAT+ 13-TOPS ~$63, 25-TOPS ~$90; Radxa AICore module ~$187. No per-device licensing surfaced.

Endo take: Strong buy/partner candidate: cheap, low-power, Pi-compatible NPU modules let Endo add on-device vision/anomaly AI to sensors without designing silicon. Best fit of the edge-AI options for Endo's cost-sensitive, low-power form factor.

Pricing: Chip/module + DevKit hardware sales plus Palette software; DevKit 2.0 via online store, enterprise pricing on request.

Endo take: Partner/watch rather than core buy: excellent for heavier edge-vision or robotics gateways, and its L&T tie-up gives an India on-ramp, but sub-10W is still high for battery soil sensors. Better fit for Endo's ETP/STP industrial edge boxes than field nodes.

LoRa / connectivity

SateliotcompanyNEW
Capability 5Ease 3Momentum 5Combined 13/15

Barcelona-based LEO satellite operator delivering standards-based 5G NTN NarrowBand-IoT (3GPP Release 17) connectivity, letting standard NB-IoT devices roam to satellite where there is no cellular coverage. Targets agriculture, livestock, utilities and environmental sensing.

Backers & Funding: Backed by the Government of Spain (~18.7% stake); investors include Global Portfolio Investments, Cellnex (early), and public co-financing partners. Co-founder/CEO Jaume Sanpera. Total ~$77.7M historically; closed EUR70M Series B (March 2025) and launched a EUR100-116M public/private Series C (April 2026) with up to ~50% public match-funding to deploy the constellation.

Traction: Six satellites launched, five more planned in 2026; full constellation targeted 2027/2028. ~400 clients in 60 countries and ~EUR270M in precontracts; operator deals with Telefonica, Deutsche Telekom, Telenor IoT (May 2026), Turkcell demo. Won GSMA Foundry x ESA 6G Innovation Challenge (March 2026).

Reviews: Positive: genuine standards-based (no proprietary modems) NB-IoT-over-satellite, strong telco partner roster, explicit agriculture/remote focus. Negative: pre-full-constellation, so coverage today is intermittent (store-and-forward, not real-time until 2027/2028); capital-intensive and partly government-dependent; no confirmed India deployment yet.

Pricing: Wholesale connectivity sold via MNO/MVNO partners 'at a fraction of current satellite prices'; up to 50% subsidy on agriculture projects. No public per-device tariff.

Endo take: Strategic partner for Endo's rural reach: standards-based NB-IoT satellite could give Endo soil/ETP sensors connectivity in Indian fields with no cellular, but it's accessed via MNO partners and full coverage isn't until ~2027/2028. Watch and pilot, don't depend on yet.

Copenhagen-based cellular IoT connectivity provider with its own cloud-native core network and a global non-steered SIM/eSIM that auto-connects to the strongest of ~650-700 carrier networks across 180+ countries, with native cloud (AWS) integrations and traffic visibility.

Backers & Funding: Founded 2012; investors include Verdane (lead), Maersk Growth, People Ventures, and the Danish Growth Fund. ~$31.5M total over 9 rounds; largest disclosed is a $21M Series A (June 2022) led by Verdane. No larger round confirmed since.

Endo take: Easy drop-in buy for Endo's cellular-connected sensors: a single global SIM with non-steered roaming and AWS integration simplifies multi-region deployments, though for India-only fleets a local LPWAN/cellular MVNO may undercut its above-average per-SIM cost.

Funding & Market Pulse

Money this run is flowing to two poles: (1) connectivity & device-management infrastructure for hardware builders — Blues ($25M, Sequoia), Memfault (acquired by Nordic), DEEPX ($80M Series C), Sateliot (EUR100M+ Series C) — and (2) India-rooted and Global-South soil-carbon MRV — Varaha ($20M Series B, WestBridge) and Miraterra (~$16M CAD + Trace Genomics buy). Pure soil-sensing hardware, by contrast, is consolidating (CropX's 7th acquisition) and seeing thinner fresh equity, signaling that value is migrating up-stack from sensors to the carbon/agronomy software they feed.

Build vs Buy

  • Buy/partner the connectivity & fleet layer, don't build it. Blues Notecard (satellite-capable) plus Memfault OTA/observability (free to ~$0.10/device/mo, now bundled with Nordic nRF) would give Endo production-grade plumbing in weeks — the only gate is whether the ~$49 Notecard BOM fits ultra-low-cost smallholder units.
  • Buy edge-AI silicon as a module (DEEPX), don't design it. A ~$63-90 Pi-compatible 13-25 TOPS NPU lets Endo add on-device anomaly/vision AI to ETP/STP gateways without touching silicon; reserve SiMa.ai for heavier industrial edge boxes where its L&T India tie-up helps.
  • Partner on MRV, build the ground-truth sensor. Varaha and Boomitra are channels, not rivals: Endo should supply cheap, validated soil/SOC measurement into their pipelines. Yard Stick's SHI-validated ~45cm accuracy is the bar Endo's carbon hardware must clear to be credible.
  • Build the low-cost niche the leaders won't serve. CropX, Stenon and TerraBlaster all aim at mechanized US/EU farms with high-cost optical/LIBS hardware; the smallholder-India price point and the ETP/STP water vertical (where DigitalPaani is the only real peer, at just $1.2M seed) remain Endo's defensible build space.
